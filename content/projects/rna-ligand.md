---
title: "Cross‑Domain Multimodal Contrastive Learning for RNA–Ligand Binding Affinity"
date: 2024-01-01
---

Built a CLIP‑style framework integrating RNA sequence, ligand SMILES (RiNALMo, SMI‑TED), and 3D structural features; achieved **Test RMSE ~1.68** vs 1.85 benchmark. Includes structure‑based clustering (RNA3DB, RMalign) and pocket extraction (RDKit, Biopython, PyMOL).
